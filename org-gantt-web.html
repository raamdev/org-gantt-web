Mapping: phases are top-level headings; each child task is a ** heading with its own SCHEDULED: (start) and DEADLINE: (end). The phase heading carries no timestamps — its span, progress, and [2/6] cookie are derived from its children, so it can never drift out of sync. Milestones are headings tagged :milestone: with a DEADLINE:, and work at either level. Keywords flip to DONE at 100%. The file opens cleanly in Emacs: agenda, clocking, and archiving all work.
